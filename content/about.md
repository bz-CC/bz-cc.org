## Who we are

The bz Computer Club (bz-CC) is a non-profit association based in Fribourg, Switzerland. We help people and organizations get the most out of technology, offering IT services, training, and practical know-how for the benefit of the community.

## What we do

We provide IT services for individuals, non-profit organizations, and public institutions, including consulting, support, setting up and operating IT systems, and help with data protection and IT security. We also offer training, courses, and workshops, create freely accessible information material on IT topics, support projects in the field of open technologies, and collaborate with other organizations.

## Get involved

Interested in what we do? Take a look at our [projects](/projects) to see our work in action, or [get in touch](/contact) with us directly. We’re happy to hear from you.
