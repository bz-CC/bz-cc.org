## Intro

[Sputim](https://www.youtube.com/@sputim) is a Swiss internet content creator, active since the early days of YouTube. Over the years, a lot of content and a loyal audience have built up around the name, and the website is the home base for all of it. Our association takes care of hosting and maintaining that web presence.

## WordPress as the foundation

The site runs on WordPress. It is flexible, well understood, and lets content be published without touching any code. The trade-off is that a public WordPress site needs regular attention, which is exactly the part we take off Sputim's plate.

## Hosting and patching

We host the site ourselves, so we control the environment it runs in. Patching is a core part of the job: WordPress core, themes, plugins, and the underlying system are kept up to date, because outdated components are the most common way public sites get compromised. Updates are applied on a regular basis rather than whenever someone remembers.

## Backups

Patching reduces the risk, backups cover what remains. We take regular backups of the site, so that after a failed update, a defective plugin, or a compromise, it can be restored to a known good state instead of being rebuilt from scratch. Years of content are not something to leave to chance.

## DNS and domain management

We also manage the domain sputim.ch and its DNS records. Keeping the domain, DNS, and hosting in one place means changes happen quickly and without coordination overhead, and nothing falls through the cracks, such as an expiring domain or a forgotten record.

## Support

If something breaks or needs changing, there is a single point of contact. Sputim can focus on creating content, and we make sure the platform it is published on keeps running.

## The result

A reliable, up-to-date, and backed-up web presence for a creator who has been around since the early days of YouTube, hosted, patched, and supported by us.
